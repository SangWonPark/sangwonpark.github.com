**Cooking Log** ("the App") respects your privacy and **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.**

### 1. On-Device Local Storage Only
- All recipes, ingredients, step photos, cooking logs, profile details (nickname, bio, avatar image), and app settings (theme, language) created in the App are stored **exclusively on your device** using Apple's `SwiftData` and `UserDefaults`.
- Neither the developer nor any third party has access to your data.

### 2. Photo Library Access (Photos Picker)
- The App uses Apple's system `PhotosPicker` solely to let you attach photos to your recipes and profile avatar.
- Selected images remain locally inside the App's container on your device and are never uploaded over the network.

### 3. No Third-Party Sharing, Ads, or Tracking
- The App does not require user registration or sign-in.
- The App contains no advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or telemetry.
- Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored data from your device.
